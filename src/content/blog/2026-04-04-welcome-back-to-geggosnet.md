Hello friends and welcome back to everyone's favourite website! It's been a while since [Geggos.net](http://Geggos.net) had any activity but I'm glad to say it's back. Despite it being back, you might be wondering why the website looks completely different.. Well, the website has been completely rebuilt from the ground up, with help from my new tech support friend Alex Buh! The website isn't just visually different but the entire backend technical side has been remade to work much more efficiently.

So, here's a little walk through of each section of [Geggos.net](http://Geggos.net) v2 and what you can expect going forward.

#### Home Page

The Home page of [Geggos.net](http://geggos.net) is probably the most different aspect of the website, gone are the six buttons that lead to different pages, now basic information about Geggos is located here. This includes my discord status, current time, the most recent movie I've watched and my latest project/blog post. This way you can see all the important stuff the second you open the website.

#### Blog

The Blog page looks pretty similar, but works a lot smoother this time around! The posts are written with a different system which means I have more freedom with stuff like image placement and more ways to make my text even more fun to read, like making it **BOLD** or using highlights!! I can even do a list now, check it out:

- See?
- I'm making a list
- Isn't that awesome?  
Pretty cool right? There is so many different things I can do now, a lot of it I don't understand so hopefully I figure it out as I write more blog posts. Speaking of more posts, I'm going to aim to try and write at least one post a month. I won't make any promises though, since there might be a few months I just have nothing to write about.

#### Guestbook

This is the newest part of the website and probably the most fun, as it lets everyone have their own little mark on the website. All you have to do is write a message, pick some fun emojis, send it, and I'll respond when I see it! If you have any suggestions for new emojis you can also suggest that.

#### Projects

The project page is a work in progress. Not much is on there right now, but you can expect to see more and more slowly revealed over time when I'm ready to show fun stuff. I promise this time around, I will actually update my project page.. eventually.

#### Gallery

Very similar to the original gallery with artwork done by Halfcharr and Cheffic so far. The gallery is split into three subcategories currently with Comics, Christmas and a new one, Misc. Misc will include anything that does not fit into other subcategories like the website logo and titles. More galleries will come in the future.

#### Archives

The Archives is another new page that will be used when a gallery is outdated, or a project is completed. Currently, only Pixelmon/Cobblemon are located here, but more will be moved here in the future.

### That's all for now

Currently, that's everything on [Geggos.net](http://Geggos.net) but more will be added in the future, I will try to keep up with writing blog posts probably related to things I am interested in like new movie or game releases. I will also make an effort to rewrite the two older blog posts from the original version of [Geggos.net](http://geggos.net) and repost them here shortly. I hope you enjoyed this little post welcoming you back, whether you are my friend, someone I've never met, or a stalker who's in love with me, you are welcome to enjoy [Geggos.net](http://Geggos.net) to your hearts content, because this time, hopefully, it's staying for good!!!
